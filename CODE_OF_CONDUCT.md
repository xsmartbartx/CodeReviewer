# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a documentation project. Interactions here are about links, configuration files, and structure — there is very little to fight about, and we would like to keep it that way.

Expected behavior:

- **Assume competence.** A contributor who got something wrong usually had a reason. Ask before correcting.
- **Critique the contribution, not the contributor.** "This link duplicates the entry above" is useful. "Did you even read the README" is not.
- **Be specific.** Vague objections waste everyone's time. If you want a change, say what change.
- **Accept the scope.** Some good ideas do not belong here. A decline is about fit, recorded in [`docs/vision.md`](docs/vision.md), not about the person.
- **Disagree in public and in writing.** Structural disagreements become issues or ADRs, not private lobbying.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any venue connected to this project.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including in configuration examples and link descriptions.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Deliberately misleading contributions: undisclosed affiliate links, resources misrepresented in their description, or content submitted to manipulate rankings.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or the review process.

## Scope

This applies to pull requests, issues, discussions, commit messages, review comments, and any project-related communication in other venues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, decline, or remove any contribution that violates these standards, and may block accounts that continue after a warning.

To report a problem, open an issue if it is safe to do so publicly, or contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled discreetly, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed to the person reported without their consent.

Enforcement is proportionate: a warning first for a first-time, non-malicious issue; removal of contributions and blocking for repeated or deliberate violations.
